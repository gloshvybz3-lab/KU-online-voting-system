Kampala University Online Voting System enforces the architectural principle of Absolute Ballot Secrecy. The system records your participation in Table A (voter_participation) to ensure you can only vote once. Simultaneously, your vote selections are stored in Table B (votes) without any reference, foreign key, or correlation back to your Student ID. Furthermore, ballots are shuffled in batch storage to prevent timing or sequential tracking.

The Electoral Commission establishes eligibility rules for contested offices. University-wide positions (such as Guild President) are visible to all students across all campuses. Campus-specific positions (e.g. Luweero Campus Representative) or faculty-specific positions (e.g. Faculty of Computer Science Representative) are dynamically filtered so you only see the positions you are authorized to elect.

After you submit your ballot, the system produces a unique cryptographic token (e.g., KU-XXXX-XXXX). You can verify this token at any time on the "Verify Receipt" portal to confirm that your vote was officially acknowledged by the Electoral Commission, without compromising ballot secrecy.

Real-time vote tabulations are maintained on a secure administrative ledger. Once the voting deadline passes and the Electoral Commission completes its verification procedures, the EC Chairperson formally approves and publishes the official results on the public portal.
